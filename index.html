About the Chapter

More Than a Student Club

The IEI MGM Student Chapter is a student-led body affiliated with The Institution of Engineers (India) at MGM's College of Engineering, Nanded.

The chapter provides a platform for engineering awareness, technical learning, knowledge-sharing, student engagement and professional development — complementing classroom education through activities where students learn from faculty, peers and engineering professionals.
