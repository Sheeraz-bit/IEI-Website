About the Chapter

More Than a Student Club

IEI MGM is a chapter of the Institution of Engineers (India) at MGM's College of Engineering, Nanded.

It provides an engineering-focused platform for technical learning, professional development, and collaboration — connecting students with each other, with faculty, and with the wider engineering community.
