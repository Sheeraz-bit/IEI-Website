About IEI MGM

The IEI Student Chapter at MGM's College of Engineering, Nanded is a student-led body affiliated with the Institution of Engineers (India).

The chapter exists to bridge classroom learning with professional engineering practice. It gives students a structured platform to engage with technical activities, workshops, expert sessions, and project-based learning — while being part of a national engineering community.

Through its activities, the chapter encourages collaboration, leadership, technical curiosity, and continuous professional development among student engineers.
